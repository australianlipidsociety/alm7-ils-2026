Pre-conference workshop

ANPC Workshop

A practical introduction to NMR lipoprotein profiling and LC-MS lipidomics — from study design and sample handling through to quality control, interpretation and reporting.

ANPC website ↗
Meet the facilitators

Workshop Facilitators

Tap a facilitator to read their bio.

Workshop timetable

Agenda

Session 1

Platforms, Study Design & Sample Collection

09:00–09:10
Welcome & Overview
Workshop aims, ANPC introduction and facilitator overview.
09:10–09:35
What Do These Platforms Actually Measure?
How NMR lipoprotein profiling and LC-MS lipidomics differ, complement each other and support different biological questions.
09:35–10:00
Study Design Considerations
Matrix choice, fasting status, sample size, longitudinal versus cross-sectional designs and integrating lipid measurements into existing studies.
10:00–10:25
Sample Collection & Handling
Collection tubes, processing, pre-analytical effects, freeze-thaw cycles, long-term storage and biobanked samples.
10:25–10:50
Running Large Human Studies
Batch planning, randomisation, sample tracking, working with core facilities, budgeting and realistic timelines.
10:50–11:00
Discussion & Open Questions
Questions and take-home points from Session 1.
11:00–11:30
Lab Tour & Morning CoffeeTour the ANPC NMR and LC-MS facilities, sample preparation areas and data infrastructure.
Session 2

Data Processing, Quality Assessment & Biological Interpretation

11:30–12:20
From Raw Output to Analysis-Ready Data
NMR processing and IVDR outputs; LC-MS peak detection and lipid annotation; pooled QC design, run-order effects, drift and correction.
12:30–12:50
Common Pitfalls & How to Catch Them
Batch effects, haemolysis, lipaemia, sample mix-ups, extraction failures and other red flags worth investigating.
12:50–13:15
From Data to Biology
Interpreting lipoprotein subclasses and lipid classes, selecting biologically meaningful features, visualisation and reporting.
13:15–13:30
Open Discussion & Wrap-Up
Bring your own study questions, review key takeaways and explore ANPC services and further resources.
